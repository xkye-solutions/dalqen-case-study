Discussion is for people: comments, @mentions, replies, and chips that point at an artwork version. Activity is the order’s record: stage moves, handoffs, artwork decisions, QC results and the release, grouped and credited to whoever did them.

The two never mix. A comment can’t be mistaken for a decision, and a decision is never buried between comments.

<Decisions>

- **Discussion is human-only.** The system never posts there, so every line in it was written by a person.
- **Mentions only offer people who can see the order.** Nobody gets pulled into a job they can’t open.
- **Activity is written by what people do.** Nobody types into it, so it can be trusted as the history.

</Decisions>

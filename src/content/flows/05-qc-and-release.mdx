Overall QC is recorded per lineup item. Jun from Printing has passed 19 of the 20 jerseys, and number 7 is the one left.

When #7 fails with an issue, the order stays in Overall QC and the 19 that passed stay passed. After the repair, #7 is rechecked and passes, and the order becomes Ready for Pickup.

Release is for the whole order. The front desk records who received it, and is asked for the manual Payment signal, which never blocks the handover.

<Decisions>

- **A fail belongs to one item.** It holds the order in QC without undoing anyone else’s passes.
- **A recheck keeps the history.** Fail, then Pass, both stay on #7’s record.
- **Release needs a name.** “Received by” is required. Payment is a signal, not a gate.

</Decisions>

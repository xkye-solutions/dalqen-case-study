Every upload is a numbered Artwork version, and the client’s decision is recorded against that version. Paolo in Layout has uploaded v1.

The coach rejects it: bigger numbers, and the sponsor logo goes on the back. The order moves to Revision, a stage everyone can see. v2 comes back with bigger numbers, is approved, and becomes the one **Current Approved** version.

<Decisions>

- **Exactly one Current Approved.** Every version is kept, but only one is the version Printing works from.
- **A rejection needs a reason.** The client’s words stay with the version they were about.
- **Rulers measure the print, not the screen.** Edge rulers and the grid use the file’s physical size, in inches or centimetres, and zooming never changes a measurement.
- **No size, no ruler.** A logo file without a physical size says “Ruler unavailable for this file” instead of guessing.

</Decisions>
